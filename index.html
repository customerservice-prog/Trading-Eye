Beginner guide

How to read Trading Eye

1
Start with the chart

The candles are stored real provider bars. The shaded area appears only after the server model has enough real history; it is a probability, not a guarantee.

2
Check the AI Market Brain

UP, FLAT, and DOWN are probabilities. Confidence tells you how strongly the model believes its current read.

3
Read the reasons

Trading Eye derives features only from stored provider data and explains them in plain English so you know exactly what the model used.

4
Watch AI Learning

The learning tab is the audit trail. Predictions are stored in Postgres first, graded after their horizon, and cannot be rewritten with hindsight.

5
Keep it paper-only

The Buy/Sell buttons use paper money and simulated fills, but their reference prices must come from the real feed. Real execution remains unavailable.

Nothing on this screen guarantees a market outcome.
